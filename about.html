About us

We built StudySpot to end the room hunt

We are two students who were tired of walking between buildings to find an empty study room. StudySpot is our course project for CPIS-358.

  • Mission
  • Team
  • How we built it
  • Numbers

Our mission

Make every study room on campus easy to find, fair to share and quick to book, so students spend their time learning instead of searching.

What we believe

  • Shared spaces work best when everyone can see what is free.
  • Booking a room should take less than a minute.
  • Cancelling a room you will not use gives it to another student.

How we built it

  1. Chose the topic: study room booking for campus.
  2. Picked the look: deep green with warm orange, suggested by Wejdan.
  3. Built the pages: Home, Login, Sign Up and About Us with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  4. Tested and published the site with GitHub Pages.
