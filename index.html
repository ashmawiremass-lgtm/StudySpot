About the idea

Why StudySpot?

The problem and our answer

Before exams, students walk from building to building asking whether a room is free. Groups lose time, and some rooms stay empty because nobody knew they were available.

StudySpot shows every room, its size and its free start times in one place. Choose a room type, how long you need it and a date, and book in a few taps.

What you get

  • Live view of free start times for every room
  • Rooms for 1 to 12 people, with their equipment listed
  • Booking in under a minute, cancel any time
  • Works on phones, tablets and laptops

How it works

  1. Create an account with your KAU email, or use the demo account.
  2. Search by room type, duration and date.
  3. Tap a start time on the room you like and confirm.
  4. Find it under My bookings. You get a reminder before it starts, and can add it to your calendar or cancel.
